About Me

I am passionate about aviation and love to fly!

I have always been interested in flying. I grew up hearing stories from family and with a passion for adventure. In 2023 I decided to pursue a professional career as a pilot.

I began my aviation journey through a Part 141 flight school program, earning my Private, Instrument, Commercial, Multi-Engine, CFI, and CFII certificates and ratings. My training taught me the importance of preparation, discipline, sound decision-making, and maintaining a high standard of safety in every flight.

Today, I have the privilege of sharing what I’ve learned with others. As a flight instructor I believe great flight instruction goes beyond teaching someone how to control the aircraft. It is about developing good judgment, building confidence, understanding the why behind each decision. I prepare students to operate safely and independently.
