LEGAL

Terms of Service

Below are listed the terms of service for flight instruction.

01

Cancellation Policy

I understand that schedules change, and I will always do my best to accommodate rescheduling when possible.

24-HOUR NOTICE

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice when canceling or rescheduling a lesson, flight review, IPC, or discovery flight. Cancellations made with at least 24 hours’ notice will not incur a cancellation fee.

LATE CANCELLATIONS

Cancellations or rescheduling requests made with less than 24 hours’ notice may be subject to a fee of 50% of the scheduled CFI instruction fee.

NO-SHOW

If you do not arrive for a scheduled lesson or flight and do not provide notice, the full scheduled CFI instruction fee may be charged.

02

Weather and Safety Policy

Saftey is the number one priority. It is an essential part of learning to be a pilot. It is a variable that we need to be aware of and plan for.

BAD WEATHER

There is NO cancellation fee when a flight is canceled or rescheduled due to weather.

PILOT SAFETY

There is NO cancellation fee when a flight is canceled or rescheduled due to a pilot safety concern. Life happens. It's ok to cancel or reschedule if you aren't feeling like you can fly safely.

AIRCRAFT SAFETY

There is NO cancellation fee when a flight is canceled or rescheduled due to an aircaft safety concern. All flights will conduct a pre-flight inspection of the aircraft which is a thorough physical check of an aircraft and its documents to ensure it is safe and legal to fly.

03

Payment Policy

Payment is due at the end of each lesson. It will be determined by the amount of time that instruction was given.

PAYMENT METHODS

Accepted payement methods are Zelle, Venmo, or Cash

NON-PAYEMENT & SUSPENSION OF SERVICE

In the event that any payment is not successfully received for services rendered, there will be no more instruction given until the balance is settled. We reserve the right to temporarily suspend or permanently terminate your access to the services, without liability, until all outstanding balances, including any applicable late fees or collection costs, are paid in full.

LATE FEES

Lack of payment for instruction services rendered will result in suspension of services and may be subject to a fee of 50% of the CFI instruction cost owed for the lesson. Services will resume once payment is recieved.

04

Aircraft & Rental

Aircraft rentals are billed separately from the contract instructor rates. The aircraft owner, (flight school, club, FBO, or other), sets aircraft rental rates and terms.

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES

The student is responsible for the rental costs and insurance terms that are determined by the aircraft owner.

AIRCRAFT DAMAGES

State who is responsible for damage to a rented aircraft during instruction.

07

Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated from time to time, and that continuing to book lessons after an update means the student accepts the current version.
