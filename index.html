Baker Flight Instruction

Learn to Fly with Confidence.

Flight Training is more than just learning how to fly an aircraft. It is about developing the skills, knowledge, and confidence to become a safe and capable pilot.

Baker Flight Instruction provides professional and personalized contract flight instruction that is designed to help each student understand not just know what to do, but why they're doing it. From your first lesson through advanced training, the goal is to build strong fundamentals, sound decision-making, and a lasting passion for aviation.

Whether you're taking your first flight or working toward your next certificate, let's take the next step together.
